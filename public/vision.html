Vision & mission

Every woman, everywhere, should have help within reach, at any hour.

Our vision

To become the global safety app for women across the world, and the first to bring many layers of protection together in one place, built around the strongest volunteer response system anywhere.

We want more volunteers to join to protect, and more women to use Beti Bachao for their safety. One day, we hope Beti Bachao comes built into every new mobile phone, straight from the factory, so that no woman has to look for help when she needs it most.

Our mission

  • Make asking for help effortless. SOS by button, shake, power button, voice, blink or a wearable button, even when the phone is locked.
  • Make sure help actually comes. Alert verified volunteers nearby, keep searching until someone accepts, and escalate when anything stalls.
  • Keep women in control. Privacy by design, and an SOS that ends only when she confirms she is safe.
  • Free for women and volunteers. Always.

Languages

Beti Bachao works in 14 languages: 10 Indian languages (English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati and Urdu) and 4 international languages (Arabic, Spanish, French and Portuguese). More languages are on the way.

Research and development

  • Safety for blind and low-vision women. Spoken status, voice commands and screen-reader support are already in the app. We are researching more ways, such as vibration patterns and simpler sign-up, with blind women themselves.
  • Rewarding our best volunteers. We are designing a recognition and reward programme for top volunteers, for example every quarter, so that those who protect others are thanked and encouraged.

Partner with us

CSR partners. Beti Bachao aims to be part of corporate social responsibility initiatives: sponsoring volunteer training, safety awareness in colleges and workplaces, and rollouts in new cities.

Venture capitalists and angel investors. We are looking for investors who share our vision of taking Beti Bachao global.
