Beti Bachao launches a free women's safety app that brings verified volunteers, family and police together in one SOS

Multiple ways to raise an alert, live arrival times, and 14 languages. The app aims to become a global safety app for women.

Hyderabad, India, 27 November 2026. Beti Bachao, a free safety app for women, is now available for Android phones at betibachao24x7.com.

Most safety apps stop once a message is sent. Beti Bachao is designed to make sure help actually arrives. When a woman raises an SOS, the app alerts verified volunteers near her, searching in widening circles until someone accepts. At the same time it sends her emergency contacts an SMS with her live location, and she can add her nearest police station as one of those contacts. She can see who is coming, how far away they are, and about how long they will take.

Key features

  • SOS by holding a button, shaking the phone, pressing the power button five times, a spoken SOS word, three deliberate blinks, or a Bluetooth keychain button
  • Volunteers verified by the Beti Bachao team on a live video call before they can respond
  • Live tracking with arrival times, and alerts that escalate if no one responds
  • A duress PIN that looks like "cancel" but quietly keeps help coming
  • Spoken guidance and screen-reader support for blind and low-vision women
  • 14 languages: 10 Indian and 4 international
  • Free for women and for volunteers

How it began

The idea came from a family conversation between Vijay Surya and his brother-in-law, Z. Vinod Kumar, about how to protect the people they love. Vijay asked why there could not be one app a woman can rely on when she feels unsafe, and set out to build it.

"A woman's safety should never depend on luck or on which app she happened to install. Our goal is simple: when she asks for help, help should reach her. We want Beti Bachao to protect women everywhere, and one day to come built into every new phone."

Vijay Surya, Beti Bachao

What's next

Beti Bachao is researching further support for blind and low-vision women and a reward programme for its most active volunteers. Beti Bachao welcomes CSR partners, venture capitalists and angel investors who want to help take the app to more cities and countries.

About Beti Bachao

Beti Bachao is a free safety app for women, based in Hyderabad, India. It connects a woman in danger with verified volunteers nearby, her family and the police, and stays with her until she confirms she is safe.
