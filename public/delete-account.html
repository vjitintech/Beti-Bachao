Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open the Beti Bachao app and log in.

Or ask us by email

Email vjitintech@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Beti Bachao account”. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, contacts, saved places, settings and device tokens — deleted.
  • Evidence audio — deleted.
  • Past SOS records — your name and phone are removed; an anonymous record (time and outcome) is kept for up to 2 years for safety statistics and legal reasons.
