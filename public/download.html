Beti Bachao for Android

Free women's safety app. Hold SOS for 3 seconds and verified volunteers nearby, your family and the police get your live location.

Download the app (14 MB)

Android 8 or newer · Early-access version before the Play Store release

Install in 4 steps

  1. Tap “Download the app”. If Chrome warns that the file may be harmful, tap Download anyway. The warning appears for every app installed outside the Play Store.
  2. Open the downloaded file from the notification, or from Files → Downloads.
  3. If your phone says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

Then, as a woman

  1. Tap Create account, choose I need safety, and enter your name, email and mobile number.
  2. On Set up your phone, keep everything on and tap Allow selected.
  3. Add 1–5 important contacts (family) under Places & contacts.
  4. In danger, hold the red SOS button for 3 seconds.

Then, as a volunteer

  1. Tap Create account and choose Volunteer.
  2. On Set up your phone, tap Allow selected so SOS alerts can ring.
  3. Request your video verification. Our trust team calls you and checks your ID on the call. Only the last 4 digits are kept.
  4. Once approved, turn On duty. Nearby SOS alerts ring loudly; tap Accept to help.
