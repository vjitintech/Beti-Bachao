Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Beti Bachao (“the app”) is provided free of charge by Endoxous International Private Limited, Flat-101, Plot-30P, Basaveswara Nilayam, Pragathi Nagar, Hyderabad, Telangana – 500090, India (CIN U01100TG2022PTC166956) (“we”). It helps a woman in danger reach her trusted contacts, nearby verified volunteers, operators and the police. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account: name, email, mobile number, password (stored only as a secure hash), language and accessibility settings.
  • Your contacts: the names and phone numbers you add as important contacts and place-based contacts, and the places you save (home, college, office).
  • Location: your phone’s location only while an SOS or “Track my travel” trip is active, and when you choose to share it. When protection is switched on, the app checks your location to pick the right contacts for the place you are at.
  • SOS records: time, status updates, who responded, and any rating you give.
  • Evidence audio: if enabled, a short audio recording (about 15 seconds) when an SOS starts. It is encrypted and deleted after 30 days unless needed for an investigation you request.
  • Volunteers: name, phone, training progress, and the last four digits of the identity document shown on the verification video call. We never store ID document images or full ID numbers.
  • Device: a notification token so we can alert you, and basic error logs.

Face detection for “3 blinks” SOS and voice detection for the help word run on your phone; camera images and voice are not sent to us.

Why we use it

  • To send your SOS, location and live-tracking link to your contacts, nearby verified volunteers, our operators and, if no one responds, the nearest police station.
  • To verify volunteers and keep everyone safe, and to investigate misuse.
  • To show anonymous statistics (for example, how many women were helped). These never identify you.

Who receives it

  • During an SOS only: your trusted contacts (by SMS with a live link), volunteers who accept your SOS (your exact location is shown only after they accept), our operators, and the police station contacted for you.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: hosting (Render / Amazon Web Services), SMS and voice (MSG91, Exotel), and push notifications (Google Firebase, Apple). They process data only on our instructions.
  • Authorities, when required by Indian law.

We never sell your data and never show advertising.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location (including in the background while an SOS or trip is active) — to share where you are with the people helping you.
  • SMS — to text your emergency contacts from your own phone when you raise an SOS, even without internet. The app does not read your messages.
  • Microphone — for your help word and the optional evidence recording. Camera — only for “3 blinks” SOS and only on your phone.
  • Notifications — to tell you when help is coming, and to alert volunteers.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. SOS records are kept for up to 2 years for safety and legal reasons, then deleted or anonymised. Evidence audio is deleted after 30 days.

Your rights

You can view and download your data (Settings → Download my data), correct it, or delete your account at any time (Settings → Delete my account, or see how to delete your account). You can withdraw consent by deleting your account.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed, evidence is encrypted, staff accounts use two-step sign-in, and every access to SOS details is recorded in a tamper-proof audit log.

Children

The app is for users aged 18 and over. Users under 18 should use it with a parent or guardian.
